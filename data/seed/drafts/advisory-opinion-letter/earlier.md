# Closing Opinion Letter — The Quillavern Equipment Facility

To Pelluvane Lending Cooperative (Lender): Veylora & Quenwick Counsel LLP (Opinion Counsel) delivers this fictional demonstration opinion concerning the proposed equipment facility for Quillavern Instruments LLC (Borrower). Opinion Counsel represents Borrower, not Lender. This letter states the opinions requested for the closing described below and the agreed terms governing Lender's receipt and permitted reliance. No real jurisdiction's law is being summarized.

## Section 1. Transaction and Scope

Borrower proposes to execute a $3,000,000 equipment credit agreement and a security agreement in favor of Lender. This opinion addresses organizational authority and enforceability under the fictional commercial law of the Territory of Lethrune. It does not address tax, insolvency priorities, or the commercial value of collateral. References to the agreements mean the execution copies delivered with this letter, rather than earlier drafts or later amendments.

## Section 2. Documents Examined

Opinion Counsel has examined Borrower's charter, operating agreement, manager resolutions, the execution copies of the credit and security agreements, and a current organizational-status certificate. Opinion Counsel has also reviewed the officer certificate identifying the signatories and material contractual restrictions. Copies have been compared with the supplied originals where available. Lender may request the document index, which identifies each examined item by title and date without disclosing unrelated privileged communications.

## Section 3. Factual Inquiry

For factual matters, Opinion Counsel has relied on the officer certificate after making reasonable inquiry of Borrower's responsible managers. Opinion Counsel has not conducted an independent audit or valuation. No reliance is placed on a statement known by the signing lawyer to be false. The phrase to our knowledge means the actual knowledge of the lawyers principally handling this transaction after the inquiry expressly described in this section.

## Section 4. Organizational Opinions

Borrower is validly organized and has organizational power to enter the agreements and grant the described security interest. The manager resolutions authorize execution, delivery, and performance, and the identified signatories are duly authorized. These opinions concern organizational action and do not determine whether Borrower will remain solvent or meet future payment obligations. Opinion Counsel expresses no opinion on factual compliance with financial covenants after the closing date.

## Section 5. Enforceability Opinion

The agreements constitute binding obligations of Borrower, enforceable according to their terms, subject to generally applicable insolvency limitations and principles of equity under the fictional law identified above. A remedy may be limited where its exercise would be unconscionable or contrary to a nonwaivable obligation. Such a limitation does not, in Opinion Counsel's judgment, invalidate the agreements as a whole or prevent enforcement of their principal payment obligations.

## Section 6. Collateral Qualifications

The security agreement creates a security interest in the described equipment to the extent Borrower has rights in that equipment. Opinion Counsel gives no opinion on perfection, priority, fixtures, or property located outside Lethrune. Lender remains responsible for arranging any filing or possession necessary to protect its interest. The equipment description must be read with the signed collateral schedule; an omitted asset is not brought within this opinion by inference.

Opinion Counsel has not reviewed environmental permits, land-use approvals, or specialized operating licenses, and expresses no opinion on those subjects. The absence of an exception elsewhere in this letter shall not be understood as an assurance concerning an expressly excluded regulatory subject.

## Section 7. Reliance and Distribution

Only Lender may rely on this letter in connection with the initial advance. Lender may furnish copies to its auditors and regulators for information, without creating reliance rights. No borrower affiliate, participant, or subsequent assignee may rely without Opinion Counsel's written consent. A permitted recipient shall receive the complete letter, including all qualifications. Quoting an isolated conclusion does not expand the scope of an opinion or eliminate a stated limitation.

## Section 8. Date and Subsequent Events

The opinions speak as of the closing date shown on the signed delivery copy. Opinion Counsel shall notify Lender within five business days if, during the following thirty days, the signing lawyer learns that a material closing-date factual assumption was false. This limited notification duty does not require monitoring Borrower's business or researching later legal developments. A new advance or amendment requires a separate determination of whether a new opinion is needed.

## Section 9. Delivery Terms and Fees

The agreed fee for preparation and delivery is $8,000, payable by Lender upon acknowledged delivery as a closing expense. There is no contractual monetary cap on Opinion Counsel's liability to Lender under this letter. All elements and defenses of any claim remain otherwise applicable. Questions shall be sent to opinions@veyloraquenwick.example.invalid and closings@pelluvane.example.invalid. An amendment to these delivery terms requires both parties' signatures.

## Section 10. Acceptance

Opinion Counsel shall sign the final letter, and Lender shall acknowledge the delivery terms by countersignature. Electronic copies may be retained as originals. Lender's acknowledgment does not certify Borrower's facts or waive a claim arising from an inaccurate opinion. This letter and the examined execution documents define the subject of the requested opinions; informal closing communications do not enlarge the stated scope or authorized class of recipients.
