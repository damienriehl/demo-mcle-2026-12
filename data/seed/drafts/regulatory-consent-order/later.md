# Consent Order — The Thistlevane Metering Review

This fictional demonstration consent order is entered by the Orivellan Resource Assurance Commission (Commission) and Thistlevane Reclamation LLC (Operator). The Commission administers the fictional Orivellan Resource Ledger Code. Following a review of process-water records at Operator's blending facility, the parties consent to the obligations below without an adjudication of the disputed inspection findings or an admission of intentional misconduct.

## Section 1. Scope and Findings

The Commission alleges that Operator failed to reconcile discharge-meter readings with monthly process logs during the previous reporting quarter. Operator disputes the reliability of the inspection estimate but accepts the corrective duties in this order. This order concerns only the metering and recordkeeping practices at the blending facility identified in the parties' signed facility description. It does not approve any additional discharge or excuse future compliance with the Ledger Code.

## Section 2. Corrective Work

Operator shall install a calibrated replacement meter within thirty days after the effective date. A qualified technician independent of the meter vendor shall certify installation and provide the calibration results to both parties. Operator shall maintain the existing monitoring system until the replacement is commissioned. The Commission shall identify any deficiency in the certification with enough detail for Operator to propose a correction without repeating properly completed work.

## Section 3. Reporting

Operator shall submit a compliance report every calendar month for one year after installation. Each report shall include meter totals, reconciliation worksheets, and an explanation of unexplained variances above five percent. An authorized manager shall certify that the report is complete to the best of that manager's knowledge after reasonable inquiry. Reports shall distinguish measured values from estimates and explain the method used for each estimate.

## Section 4. Inspection Access

Commission staff may inspect the relevant equipment and records during ordinary operating hours upon ten business days' written notice. Operator shall provide a knowledgeable escort and reasonable safety equipment. Inspectors shall comply with posted safety requirements and avoid unnecessary interruption of production. Nothing in this access provision limits an inspection power independently available under the fictional Ledger Code, and any disagreement about such a power is reserved.

## Section 5. Administrative Payment

Operator shall pay an administrative penalty of $24,000 within thirty days after the effective date. The payment resolves the alleged recordkeeping violations described in Section 1 through that date. Payment does not purchase a right to violate future requirements. The Commission shall provide a written receipt and apply the payment solely to the obligations addressed by this order, rather than to any unrelated assessment.

## Section 6. Stipulated Assessments

A missed corrective-work deadline incurs a stipulated assessment of $1,000 per day, beginning after written notice and a seven-day cure period. The Commission shall identify the missed obligation and explain its calculation. Operator may contest whether the deadline was missed or the calculation is accurate through the dispute procedure below. No stipulated assessment accrues while an approved written extension remains in effect for the same work.

## Section 7. Extensions and Disputes

Operator may request a reasonable extension for circumstances beyond its control by explaining the cause, mitigation efforts, and proposed completion date. The Commission shall respond within fifteen business days after receiving a complete request. Silence does not approve an extension. Before contested enforcement, designated representatives shall confer within ten business days after a dispute notice, except where immediate action is authorized to prevent an imminent release.

## Section 8. Completion and Release

After receiving the final compliance report, the Commission shall issue a completion notice within thirty days if Operator has met every express obligation. That notice ends the special reporting and stipulated-assessment provisions of this order. It does not extinguish duties arising independently under the Ledger Code. Any deficiency notice shall identify the particular unfinished task and permit Operator to submit documentation showing prior completion.

## Section 9. Communications

Formal submissions shall be sent to compliance@thistlevane.example.invalid and orders@orivellan.example.invalid with confirmation of receipt. The parties shall use a shared index identifying each submission by title and date. An informal staff comment does not amend this order. A modification requires a written instrument signed by the Commission's authorized official and Operator's authorized manager. Each party shall retain a complete copy of the signed modification.

The Commission shall protect process formulas and customer-specific production data designated confidential by Operator, except as disclosure is required by the Ledger Code. Before a permitted disclosure, the Commission shall give Operator prompt notice where lawful and an opportunity to identify segregable confidential material.

## Section 10. Effective Date

This order becomes effective when both authorized representatives sign it. The parties may exchange electronic signature copies and retain them as originals. Operator's consent concerns the resolution described here and does not constitute an admission in an unrelated matter. If a provision is held unenforceable, the parties shall confer promptly about a replacement that preserves the remaining corrective program without expanding the admitted facts.
