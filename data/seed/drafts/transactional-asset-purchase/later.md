# Asset Purchase Agreement — Vellunspur Workshop Assets

This fictional demonstration agreement is made as of October 6, 2026, between Vellunspur Fabrication LLC (Seller) and Quorrelith Components LLC (Buyer). The parties propose a sale of the workshop assets described below. This draft records their negotiated allocation of closing obligations and post-closing risk and becomes binding only when both parties sign.

## Section 1. Purchased Assets

Seller shall transfer the cutting machines, inventory, customer specifications, transferable software licenses, and workshop records listed on the signed asset schedule. Cash, tax refunds, and Seller's organizational records are excluded. Buyer assumes only the equipment maintenance contracts identified on that schedule; all other liabilities remain with Seller. Seller shall provide a readable inventory export five business days before closing for reconciliation against the physical count.

## Section 2. Purchase Price and Payment

The Purchase Price is $2,400,000, payable by Buyer at closing in immediately available funds to an account designated in a signed closing instruction. Buyer shall deposit $360,000 with the jointly selected escrow holder upon execution. The deposit will be credited against the Purchase Price and returned if a closing condition fails without Buyer's breach. Neither party may change payment instructions by an unsigned email.

## Section 3. Records and Access

Before closing, Seller shall give Buyer's advisers reasonable access to the workshop and asset records during ordinary business hours. Buyer shall schedule visits through Seller's transaction manager and avoid interrupting production. Seller may redact employee medical information and personal account credentials from copies. Access does not transfer ownership, excuse an inaccurate representation, or permit Buyer to contact customers without Seller's prior written consent.

Each party shall keep the other party's nonpublic business information confidential and use it solely to evaluate or perform this transaction. Disclosure is permitted to professional advisers bound by equivalent duties or when legally compelled, following prompt notice where lawful.

## Section 4. Closing Conditions

Closing shall occur on November 20, 2026, by exchange of signed documents and confirmation of funds. Buyer's obligation to close is conditioned on receipt of contract assignment consents for contracts exceeding $50,000 in annual charges. Seller shall deliver bills of sale, lien releases, and a certificate confirming its representations as of closing. Seller's obligation to close is conditioned on payment and delivery of Buyer's assumption agreement for the scheduled maintenance contracts.

## Section 5. Representations

Seller represents that it owns the purchased assets free of liens other than those discharged at closing, has authority to enter this agreement, and has supplied materially accurate inventory records. The representations survive for twenty-four months after closing. Buyer represents that it has authority and sufficient funds to complete the purchase. Each party shall notify the other promptly upon learning that its closing certificate would be materially inaccurate.

## Section 6. Indemnification

Seller shall indemnify Buyer for losses resulting from breaches of Seller's representations, retained liabilities, or Seller's failure to perform this agreement. Seller's aggregate indemnity liability is capped at 10% of the Purchase Price. Buyer shall indemnify Seller for losses resulting from assumed liabilities or Buyer's failure to perform. An indemnified party shall give reasonably prompt written notice and permit the indemnifying party to defend third-party claims with reasonably acceptable counsel.

## Section 7. Transition Arrangements

For sixty days after closing, Seller shall provide remote instruction on the equipment for up to eighty staff hours without additional charge. Buyer shall provide safe access and identify a single coordinator. Seller need not create new software or disclose excluded personnel records. The parties shall maintain a shared log of requests, completed instruction, and any unresolved equipment questions for the closing file.

## Section 8. Claims Procedure

A claim notice shall describe the relevant facts, the provision involved, and any reasonably available estimate of loss. Failure to estimate an amount does not invalidate a timely notice. The parties shall confer for ten business days before commencing a contested proceeding, except when immediate relief is necessary to protect assets. Neither party may settle a third-party claim by imposing an admission or nonmonetary obligation on the other without written consent.

## Section 9. Notices and Amendments

Notices shall be sent by email with confirmation of receipt to seller@vellunspur.example.invalid and buyer@quorrelith.example.invalid. A notice received outside business hours is effective the next business day. Amendments require both parties' signatures. No delay in enforcing a right waives it. This agreement and its signed schedules state the parties' entire agreement concerning the purchased assets and supersede earlier expressions of interest.

## Section 10. Counterparts

The parties may sign in counterparts and exchange electronic copies, each deemed an original. A signature delivered electronically has the same effect as a paper signature. Each signatory shall retain a complete executed copy, including schedules and closing instructions. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect to the extent they can operate consistently with the transaction described here.
