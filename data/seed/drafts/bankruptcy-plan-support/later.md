# Plan Support Agreement — The Sablequoin Restructuring

This fictional demonstration agreement is made between Sablequoin Alloyworks LLC (Debtor) and the Nimbrelith Claims Consortium (Supporting Creditor). Debtor anticipates a proceeding under the fictional Restructuring Code of the Commonwealth of Vesperune. Supporting Creditor holds the unsecured supply claim identified in the signed claim schedule. The parties seek an orderly plan process while reserving the authority of the fictional restructuring tribunal to approve or reject proposed relief.

## Section 1. Supported Plan

Subject to approval and the conditions below, the plan shall continue Debtor's specialty alloy business and distribute available cash to creditors. Supporting Creditor's allowed unsecured claim shall receive a cash distribution equal to forty percent of its allowed amount. The balance shall be discharged only upon plan effectiveness and entry of the necessary approval order. No payment may be demanded under this agreement ahead of another creditor with the same approved treatment.

## Section 2. Support Commitments

Supporting Creditor shall timely vote its scheduled claim in favor of a plan materially consistent with this agreement and shall not solicit support for an inconsistent proposal while this agreement remains effective. No voting commitment requires a vote before receipt of the approved disclosure materials. Debtor shall propose the supported treatment in good faith and provide a current plan draft sufficiently before solicitation to permit review of its consistency with these terms.

## Section 3. Case Milestones

Debtor shall seek approval of disclosure materials promptly after commencement and shall obtain a confirmation order within seventy-five days after commencement. Debtor shall circulate a written status report every two weeks describing progress against that milestone. A milestone may be extended by written agreement of both parties. An agreed extension changes only the identified deadline and does not waive another condition or a previously accrued termination right.

## Section 4. Interim Financing

Debtor may seek approval for interim financing with principal not exceeding $2,500,000. Supporting Creditor shall not oppose financing consistent with that limit and the circulated financing term sheet. Any lien, priority, or financing charge remains subject to tribunal approval and applicable protections for affected creditors. This agreement does not itself grant a lien or stipulate the value of collateral. Materially different financing terms require renewed consultation before support is requested.

## Section 5. Consultation and Information

Debtor shall provide Supporting Creditor's advisers with weekly cash forecasts and reasonable access to restructuring management, subject to appropriate protection of privileged material. Supporting Creditor shall direct requests through its designated adviser and avoid contacting operating staff without consent. Debtor shall explain a refusal to provide requested information and offer a nonprivileged summary where reasonably practicable. Information access does not authorize Supporting Creditor to direct ordinary business operations.

## Section 6. Transfers of Claims

Supporting Creditor may transfer its scheduled claim only if the transferee signs a joinder accepting the remaining support and confidentiality obligations. Debtor shall receive a copy of the joinder before the transfer becomes effective under this agreement. A transfer does not increase the allowed claim or entitle a transferee to duplicate distributions. Any transferee voting right remains subject to the disclosure and solicitation requirements applicable to the plan process.

## Section 7. Expense Reimbursement

Subject to tribunal approval, Debtor shall reimburse Supporting Creditor's documented restructuring-adviser expenses up to $20,000 in total. Supporting Creditor shall submit invoices describing the work without revealing privileged advice. Debtor may dispute an unreasonable or unrelated charge and shall explain its reason in writing. The reimbursement commitment does not grant administrative priority or require payment before the tribunal authorizes it. Each party otherwise bears its own expenses.

## Section 8. Termination Rights

Either party may terminate for the other's material breach after written notice and fifteen business days to cure. Supporting Creditor may also terminate if the confirmation milestone expires without an agreed extension or if Debtor proposes materially inconsistent treatment. Termination ends prospective support obligations but does not undo a valid transfer or excuse a prior breach. Confidentiality duties survive termination according to their terms, and no termination itself creates a right to an unapproved distribution.

## Section 9. Approval and Remedies

Debtor's obligations requiring tribunal approval are expressly conditioned on that approval, and nothing requires Debtor's managers to breach an applicable fiduciary duty. The parties shall consult before seeking an amendment required by an approval ruling. Neither party may obtain an order compelling an unlawful vote or distribution. Rights to seek otherwise available relief for a breach are preserved, subject to any stay and the tribunal's authority over the restructuring proceeding.

## Section 10. Notices and Execution

Each party shall keep restructuring forecasts and negotiation communications confidential and disclose them only to advisers bound by equivalent duties or as legally required. A party receiving a compulsory demand shall give prompt notice where lawful and reasonably cooperate in seeking protection for sensitive business information.

Notices shall be sent to restructuring@sablequoin.example.invalid and support@nimbrelith.example.invalid with confirmation of receipt. Amendments require a signed writing by both parties. This agreement may be signed in electronic counterparts, which together form one agreement. It becomes effective upon delivery of both signatures and remains subject to any required approval. The signed claim schedule is incorporated solely to identify the claim covered by the support commitment.
